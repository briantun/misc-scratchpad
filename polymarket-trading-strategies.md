# Polymarket Trading Strategies

A collection of strategies for earning profit on Polymarket prediction markets.

---

## 1. Information Edge Strategies

### Primary Research
- **Direct Source Monitoring**: Follow official announcements, press releases, court filings, and government documents before they're widely reported
- **Domain Expertise**: Leverage specialized knowledge in areas like legal proceedings, sports analytics, or political science
- **Social Media Signals**: Monitor Twitter/X, Discord, Telegram for early breaking news

### Data-Driven Analysis
- **Historical Base Rates**: Compare current market prices to historical frequencies of similar events
- **Statistical Modeling**: Build models using polling data, economic indicators, or other quantitative inputs
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Track shifts in public sentiment that may precede market moves

---

## 2. Market Inefficiency Strategies

### Arbitrage Opportunities
- **Cross-Market Arbitrage**: Find price discrepancies between Polymarket and other prediction markets (Kalshi, PredictIt, Metaculus)
- **Internal Arbitrage**: Exploit mispriced related markets (e.g., if "Event A" and "Event A before Date X" are inconsistently priced)
- **Yes/No Spread Capture**: When Yes + No prices don't sum to $1.00, capture the spread

### Mispricing Detection
- **Recency Bias Exploitation**: Markets often overreact to recent news; fade extreme moves
- **Long-Shot Bias**: Markets tend to overprice unlikely outcomes; systematically sell low-probability events
- **Favorite-Longshot Bias**: Conversely, high-probability outcomes may be underpriced

---

## 3. Timing & Liquidity Strategies

### Market Making
- **Provide Liquidity**: Place limit orders on both sides to capture the bid-ask spread
- **Inventory Management**: Balance positions to avoid directional risk while earning spread

### Event-Driven Timing
- **Pre-Event Positioning**: Enter positions before scheduled events (debates, earnings, court dates) when implied volatility is low
- **Post-News Mean Reversion**: Markets often overshoot on news; trade the reversion
- **Resolution Timing**: Buy near-certain outcomes just before resolution for small but reliable gains

---

## 4. Portfolio & Risk Management

### Diversification
- **Uncorrelated Bets**: Spread capital across markets with independent outcomes
- **Category Diversification**: Mix politics, sports, crypto, entertainment to reduce sector risk
- **Time Diversification**: Hold positions across different resolution timeframes

### Position Sizing
- **Kelly Criterion**: Size bets proportionally to your edge and bankroll
- **Fractional Kelly**: Use 1/4 or 1/2 Kelly for more conservative sizing
- **Max Position Limits**: Never risk more than X% of bankroll on a single market

### Hedging
- **Correlated Market Hedging**: If long on "Candidate A wins", consider shorts on correlated outcomes
- **Partial Exit Strategy**: Scale out of positions as prices move in your favor

---

## 5. Behavioral & Contrarian Strategies

### Contrarian Plays
- **Panic Buying/Selling**: Take the other side when markets move sharply on emotions
- **Unpopular Opinion Trading**: Identify markets where crowd sentiment diverges from fundamentals
- **Calendar Anomalies**: Look for predictable patterns around weekends, holidays, or market events

### Patience Strategies
- **Waiting for Mispricings**: Don't force trades; wait for clear opportunities
- **Avoid Overtrading**: Transaction costs and slippage erode edge quickly

---

## 6. Technical Execution

### Order Management
- **Limit Orders Only**: Avoid market orders in illiquid markets
- **Iceberg Orders**: Split large orders to avoid moving the market
- **Time-Weighted Entry**: Spread entries over time to get better average prices

### Platform Mechanics
- **Gas Optimization**: Time transactions during low-fee periods (Polygon network)
- **Understand Resolution Rules**: Read fine print carefully; edge cases can cause unexpected outcomes
- **Monitor Order Book Depth**: Assess liquidity before sizing positions

---

## 7. Specific Market Types

### Political Markets
- **Poll Aggregation Models**: Build or follow sophisticated polling models
- **Early Vote/Exit Poll Interpretation**: Understand what early data actually implies
- **Regulatory/Legal Outcome Expertise**: Deep knowledge of how agencies and courts operate

### Sports Markets
- **Line Shopping**: Compare to sportsbook odds for edge detection
- **Injury/Roster News**: Fast reaction to lineup changes
- **Weather & Conditions**: Factor in game-day conditions

### Crypto/Finance Markets
- **On-Chain Analysis**: Monitor whale movements, exchange flows
- **DeFi Protocol Knowledge**: Understand technical risks for crypto-related markets
- **Macro Indicator Tracking**: Fed decisions, economic data releases

---

## 8. Risk Warnings

- **Counterparty Risk**: Understand platform risks and custody arrangements
- **Regulatory Risk**: Prediction market legality varies by jurisdiction
- **Liquidity Risk**: May not be able to exit positions at desired prices
- **Model Risk**: Your analysis could be wrong; size accordingly
- **Black Swan Events**: Unprecedented events can invalidate historical patterns

---

## Key Principles

1. **Edge First**: Never trade without a clear thesis for why the market is wrong
2. **Discipline**: Stick to your strategy; don't chase losses or FOMO into positions
3. **Record Keeping**: Track all trades, reasoning, and outcomes for continuous improvement
4. **Continuous Learning**: Markets adapt; your strategies must evolve too
5. **Bankroll Management**: Survival is prerequisite to success

---

*Note: Trading on prediction markets involves risk of loss. These strategies are for educational brainstorming purposes.*
